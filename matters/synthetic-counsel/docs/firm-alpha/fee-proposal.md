Fixed-fee preparation and prosecution for TestCo's US patent filings,
small entity status applied.

| Service | Fixed fee |
|---|---|

## Estimates and terms

Estimated lifetime cost per patent, filing through issue: about $19,500.
Estimated program cost over the four-year plan: about $340,000.

Volume discount: 8 percent on all fixed fees, available at 12 or more
filings per quarter.

EU counterpart filings are handled by our foreign associates and are
quoted separately per country. No EU pricing is included in this proposal.

Government fees, drawings, and translations are billed at cost and are
not included in the fixed fees above.
