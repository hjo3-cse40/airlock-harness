# Firm Alpha: standard engagement terms (SYNTHETIC TEST DATA)

Received 2026-08-20 alongside the fee proposal. Long-form terms document.

## Billing and payment

Fixed fees are invoiced on filing. Payment terms are net 45 days from the
invoice date. Late amounts accrue interest at 1 percent per month. Government
fees are invoiced at cost with no markup, itemized per matter.

## Staffing

One partner and one associate are assigned to the program. Substitutions
require written notice. Paralegal support is included in the fixed fees and
is not billed separately.

## Conflicts

A conflicts check is run at intake for each new invention disclosure. If a
conflict emerges mid-matter, Firm Alpha will complete any filing already in
preparation and then transfer the matter at no transfer charge.

## Liability

Aggregate liability under this engagement is capped at $250,000 or the fees
paid in the preceding twelve months, whichever is greater. Neither party is
liable for consequential damages.

## Term and termination

Either party may terminate with 60 days written notice. Work in progress at
termination is completed and billed at the fixed-fee schedule. Files are
transferred within 15 business days of the termination date.

## Confidentiality

All invention disclosures are held in confidence indefinitely. Firm Alpha
does not use client materials for training, marketing, or publications.

## Docketing

Statutory deadlines are entered into two independent docketing systems.
Reminders issue at 90, 60, and 30 days before each deadline. The client
receives a monthly docket report listing every open deadline.
