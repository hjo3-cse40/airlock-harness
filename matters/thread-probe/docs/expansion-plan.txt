Riverside Community Garden
Expansion Plan for the Adjacent Lot
Prepared by Maya Reyes, Program Coordinator

The Lot Next Door

The lot next door has been offered to the garden on the same terms as the old lot. This plan puts it into use this season rather than holding it empty for a year. The first-season budget comes to $41,250, with work starting in June so the beds are filled and planted before the summer heat sets in.

Four Asks

First, approve the total budget below. Second, approve hiring Hollis Irrigation to run the water line from the meter to the far corner, with a spigot at the end of each row. Third, approve building the greenhouse this season; it is carried as its own line and can be removed without changing the rest of the plan. Fourth, approve starting the school program with Alder Hollow Elementary in the raised beds this season.

Proposed Budget

Water line, spigots, fittings and backfill (Hollis Irrigation): $7,650
Greenhouse, delivered, with gravel base: $10,300
Cedar raised beds and hardware: $4,900
Soil, compost and delivery: $3,150
Two tool sheds (a kit on skids and a larger walk-in): $3,950
Fence and gate repair on the shared line: $1,850
School program supplies and a weekend crew leader stipend: $3,650
Permit, drawings, signs, hoses, hand tools and seed: $2,550
Contingency: $3,250

The greenhouse line is the vendor estimate plus delivery and the gravel base, and it is the only line that can come out on its own without changing the water line, the beds, or the sheds.

Site Layout

The drawing on the back shows the gate on the street side, the in-ground plots in rows running back from the gate, the raised beds along the path nearest the gate, the two sheds on the back line, and the greenhouse in the far corner at the end of the water line. The plan calls for 32 in-ground plots and 20 raised beds in the first season, with room left along the fence for more beds later.

Timeline

June: Hollis trenches and lays the water line; volunteers build and fill the raised beds behind the crew, and the first Alder Hollow Elementary visits follow once the beds are filled.
July: Greenhouse base and frame go up; in-ground plots are tilled and staked.
August: Both sheds built on a work day; Alder Hollow Elementary visits continue in the raised beds.
September: Fall planting across the new lot; season review with the board.
